# Blog hero image prompts

Midjourney prompts used to generate the blog hero images. One per technical
category, plus a blog hero. Generated images are optimised to JPG and stored in
`public/assets/blog/{category}.jpg`.

Shared house style (appended to every prompt): *editorial photography, calm and
grounded mood, muted palette of horizon blue and warm sailcloth white with soft
teak-brown accents, soft natural overcast light, Singapore maritime setting,
minimal unhurried composition, no people's faces, no text, no logos
`--ar 16:9 --style raw`*
