### The Sourced Detail

#### South Africa operational framework

South Africa is typically the African anchor market for substantive Singapore SME operations. The market sits within sophisticated regulatory framework.

Insurance regulation operates under [Insurance Act 18 of 2017](https://www.fsca.co.za/) administered by [Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA)](https://www.fsca.co.za/) for conduct supervision and [Prudential Authority](https://www.resbank.co.za/) (within South African Reserve Bank) for prudential supervision under Twin Peaks framework.

Commercial relationships with major South African insurers (Santam, Old Mutual Insure, Hollard, Bryte, AIG South Africa, specific other insurers) and specific commercial brokers matter substantially.

Mandatory frameworks include:

**Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act (COIDA)** under [Compensation Fund](https://www.labour.gov.za/) - provides compensation for work-related injuries and diseases. Employer-only contribution at variable rates by industry classification.

**Unemployment Insurance Fund (UIF)** - contribution rate 1% employer + 1% employee on monthly remuneration up to specific ceiling.

**Skills Development Levy (SDL)** - 1% of monthly payroll for operations with annual payroll exceeding R500,000.

**Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) and tax compliance** under [South African Revenue Service (SARS)](https://www.sars.gov.za/).

Specific [Companies Act 71 of 2008](https://www.cipc.co.za/) administered by [Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC)](https://www.cipc.co.za/) creates corporate framework with specific directors' duties, specific liability provisions, and operational considerations considerations.

Specific [Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)](https://www.justice.gov.za/) administered by [Information Regulator](https://inforegulator.org.za/) creates substantive data protection framework with specific compliance discipline.

#### Kenya operational framework

Kenya represents major East African commercial scope. Insurance market sits within [Insurance Act](https://www.ira.go.ke/) framework administered by [Insurance Regulatory Authority (IRA)](https://www.ira.go.ke/).

Mandatory frameworks include **National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF)** (transitioning to Social Health Insurance Fund under Universal Health Coverage framework), **National Social Security Fund (NSSF)**, and **Workers' Compensation** under [Work Injury Benefits Act (WIBA)](https://www.labour.go.ke/).

Specific [Data Protection Act 2019](https://www.odpc.go.ke/) administered by [Office of the Data Protection Commissioner](https://www.odpc.go.ke/) creates data protection framework.

#### Nigeria operational framework

Nigeria represents major West African commercial scope. Insurance market sits within [Insurance Act 2003](https://www.naicom.gov.ng/) framework administered by [National Insurance Commission (NAICOM)](https://www.naicom.gov.ng/).

Mandatory frameworks include **Pension Reform Act** contributory pension scheme (10% employer + 8% employee), **National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA)** under [NHIA Act 2022](https://www.nhia.gov.ng/), **Industrial Training Fund (ITF)** levy (1% of payroll), and **Workers' Compensation** under [Employee's Compensation Act 2010](https://www.nsitf.gov.ng/) administered by [Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund (NSITF)](https://www.nsitf.gov.ng/).

Specific [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](https://nitda.gov.ng/) administered by Nigeria Data Protection Commission creates data protection framework.

#### Egypt operational framework

Egypt represents North African commercial scope. Insurance market sits within the [Unified Insurance Law No. 155 of 2024](https://fra.gov.eg/) framework (effective 11 July 2024, consolidating and replacing the former Insurance Regulation and Supervision Law No. 10 of 1981) administered by the [Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA)](https://fra.gov.eg/).

Mandatory frameworks include **Social Insurance Law** providing comprehensive social insurance scheme, **Health Insurance Authority** universal health insurance framework progressively implementing, and specific Workers' Compensation framework.

Specific [Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020)](https://www.constitutionalcourt.gov.eg/) creates data protection framework.

#### Ghana and Mauritius operational frameworks

Ghana operations sit within [National Insurance Commission (NIC)](https://www.nicgh.org/) framework. Mandatory frameworks include **Social Security and National Insurance Trust (SSNIT)**, **National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS)**, and Workers' Compensation framework.

Mauritius operations sit within [Financial Services Commission (FSC)](https://www.fscmauritius.org/) framework. Mauritius is often used as African regional holding jurisdiction given commercial conventions and bilateral commercial scope.
