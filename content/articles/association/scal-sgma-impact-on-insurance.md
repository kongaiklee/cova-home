> **The 60-second answer:** Safety & Green Management Assessment (SgMA) is a simplified workplace safety and environmental management audit operated by **SC2 Pte Ltd**, a subsidiary of the Singapore Contractors Association (SCAL) ([SC2 SgMA page](https://www.sc2.com.sg/what-we-do/safety-and-green-management-assessment-sgma/)). All SLOTS-registered sub-contractors must complete SgMA, or qualify for a waiver by submitting ISO 45001 (SAC) or bizSAFE Star, ISO 14001 or the latest green policy, and the latest SGSecure Representative appointment letter ([SCAL SLOTS T&Cs](https://www.scal.com.sg/membership/slots-application-tnc)). SgMA is not insurance. Chubb's and AIG's WICA proposal forms ask about a firm's safety accreditation or bizSAFE level, but neither mentions SgMA.

## The Sourced Detail

Per the [SC2 SgMA page](https://www.sc2.com.sg/what-we-do/safety-and-green-management-assessment-sgma/), SgMA is "a simplified version of the WSH Management System introduced by SCAL to enhance the WSH capabilities of the Singapore List of Trade Sub-contractors (SLOTS)." The scheme was introduced in line with the Joint MND-MOM Review Committee on Construction Safety to push safety culture into small and medium construction firms.

### How SgMA works (three stages)

Per the [SCAL SLOTS Application T&Cs](https://www.scal.com.sg/membership/slots-application-tnc):

- **Stage 1 - Training (for new SLOTS registrations).** A 2-day workshop in which each participant drafts an SgMA system ready to implement at their firm.
- **Stage 2 - Assessment Audit (for SLOTS renewal).** A site audit covering documents, physical inspection, and staff interview.
- **Stage 3 (surveillance audit).** Since 1 June 2025, SgMA certificates are valid for 3 years, with a mandatory surveillance audit on or before the 18th month from the date of issue ([SC2 SgMA Audit Request Form](https://www.sc2.com.sg/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/SgMA-Audit-Request-Form-For-Year-2025-r1.pdf)).

Per the [SC2 SgMA Audit Request Form](https://www.sc2.com.sg/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/SgMA-Audit-Request-Form-For-Year-2025-r1.pdf), the SgMA audit moved from an annual audit to a 3-year cycle from 1 June 2025, and the fee is "S$1,035.50 (inclusive of 9% GST) for a 3-year cycle."

### The waiver pathway

Per the [SCAL SLOTS T&Cs](https://www.scal.com.sg/membership/slots-application-tnc): *"For SgMA waiver, please submit: (i) ISO 45001 (SAC) or bizSAFE Star; and (ii) ISO 14001 or Latest Green Policy; and (iii) Latest Appointment letter for SGSecure Representative."*

Sub-contractors that already hold ISO 45001 (SAC), the international occupational health and safety management standard, or bizSAFE Star, together with ISO 14001 (environmental management) or a green policy and the latest SGSecure Representative appointment letter, can apply for the waiver. It's the same destination (proof of WSH capability) by a different road.

### Where SgMA touches insurance

SgMA is **not** an insurance requirement. There is no statutory rule, no MAS notice, and no underwriter mandate that says "no SgMA, no policy." But the practical reality is:

- **WICA proposal forms.** Some Singapore WICA proposal forms ask about safety accreditation: Chubb's asks whether the firm has safety management accreditation and its bizSAFE level, and AIG's asks for its bizSAFE level and whether it has ISO 9000 or ISO 14000 quality systems. Neither form mentions SgMA, and neither says how the answer affects the premium.
- **PL proposal forms.** AIG's SCARs proposal form, a combined WICA and Public Liability package for renovation and specialty contractors, asks whether the firm is accredited with bizSAFE and at what level. It does not mention SgMA or say how the answer affects the premium.
- **SLOTS registration consequences.** Without SgMA (or its waiver equivalent), a sub-contractor cannot maintain SLOTS registration. SLOTS is SCAL's list of competent sub-contractors for main contractors, consultants and developers to refer to. Since 1 June 2025, hiring or renewing construction Work Permit and S Pass holders also needs BCA CRS registration, which SCAL applies for on behalf of SLOTS firms by default (except in trade heads ES03 to ES06) unless they opt out.

### What SgMA covers

The audit checks safety policy and organisational structure, documented risk assessments, Safe Work Procedures, toolbox meetings and Permit-to-Work systems, PPE issuance, environmental protections (waste, dust, noise), and SGSecure preparedness. The deliverable is a third-party audit certificate from SC2. Per [SC2's About page](https://www.sc2.com.sg/who-we-are/about-us/), SC2 is "a certification body and auditing organisation accredited by Singapore Accreditation Council, providing Quality, Environmental, Safety & Health certification and auditing services."

## What This Means for Your Business

If you're a sub-contractor seeking SLOTS registration, SgMA, or the waiver, is a condition of registration. The cost is small relative to project value (SC2's current request form charges S$1,035.50, inclusive of 9% GST, for a 3-year cycle).

The waiver needs ISO 45001 (SAC) or bizSAFE Star, together with ISO 14001 or a green policy and the latest SGSecure Representative appointment letter. A firm that qualifies writes to SCAL instead of booking the SgMA audit, and so does not pay the audit fee.

The honest framing: SgMA is not protection. It's a discipline. Your insurance is the protection.
