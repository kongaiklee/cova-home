# Careers - open roles

This file is the ONLY thing to edit when a role opens or closes. The Careers
page (`src/pages/company/Careers.tsx`) reads it at build time; commit the change
and redeploy - nothing else moves.

Format - one role per line, four pipe-separated fields, starting `- `:

    - Job title | Team | Location | link (mailto: or an absolute URL)

Teams are the four disciplines on the page: engineering, insurance operations,
content, design. No `- ` lines below the marker means zero open roles and the
page shows its ruled empty state.
